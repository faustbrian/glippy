## Decision

Treat a final direct return as an intentional termination shim only when it is
the sole statement after an exact `testing.FailNow`, `Fatal`, or `Fatalf` call.
This exception requires a bare return. Retained work, nonfinal returns,
non-testing lookalikes, and other no-return functions keep their diagnostics.
The existing compiler-required and zero-value return-shim contracts for
value-returning functions remain unchanged.

Do not add a broad `nilness` testing-assertion exclusion. Preserve the four
intentional NATS assertions as precision evidence for a future bounded
postcondition or callee-contract design, while retaining the seven actionable
conditions.
