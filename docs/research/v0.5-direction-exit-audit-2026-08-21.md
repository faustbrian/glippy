# v0.5 Direction Exit Audit, 2026-08-21

## Decision

The local implementation has completed the seven engineering stages in the
v0.5 Clippy/PHPStan direction: typed-memory reduction, incremental workspace
analysis, multi-target analysis, project semantic contracts, state-transition
correctness, curated strictness profiles, and deliberate opt-in catalog growth.

The engineering and delivery gates are complete. Canonical repository
synchronization, the selected `go-libraries/pkg/prompts` integration, exact-
candidate CI, and the native release workflow are proven below. The milestone
is not release-complete because the maintainer's required final pre-tag review
remains open. No tag or publication is authorized by this audit.

The audit began at local Glippy revision
`e06ffc160bc19fb432ef77bc9da523e7a99fb8e6`. At that point local `main` was 56
commits ahead of `origin/main`. Both public repositories,
`faustbrian/gox` and `faustbrian/glippy`, exposed `main` at
`7cfcaf4b06484c09ddbcdfd6e80284b52eed4d84`.

## Remaining Product Boundaries

The following are useful continuations but do not invalidate the implemented
v0.5 engineering contracts:

- cgo-generated inputs conservatively fall back to complete LSP package loads;
- runtime custom analyzers remain intentionally unsupported until a stable,
  statically compiled extension contract is justified;
- network and userspace filesystems and forced power-loss durability remain
  outside the supported atomic write/fix boundary; and
- newer interprocedural aliases, dynamic dispatch, recursion, and ambiguous
  package variants remain conservative rather than producing speculative
  correctness findings.
