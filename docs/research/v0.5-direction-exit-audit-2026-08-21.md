# v0.5 Direction Exit Audit, 2026-08-21

## Decision

The local implementation has completed the seven engineering stages in the
v0.5 Clippy/PHPStan direction: typed-memory reduction, incremental workspace
analysis, multi-target analysis, project semantic contracts, state-transition
correctness, curated strictness profiles, and deliberate opt-in catalog growth.

The earlier delivery evidence is retained below, but successive independent
reviews of the subsequent candidate found material gaps. Local corrections now
cover aggregate process-tree RSS accounting and interruption ownership, bounded
LSP supersession and shutdown, external replacement invalidation, conservative
RWMutex and WaitGroup reasoning, target-matrix suppression and partial-result
accounting, baseline and changed-code policy on incomplete output, catalog
admission, and historical evidence attribution. Those corrections require fresh
safe local verification, independent source review, and exact-candidate CI.
Runtime RSS, signal, interruption, and descendant-termination probes are
excluded because they are not safe to execute on the development host; current
aggregate RSS and process-containment behavior therefore remain unproven. No
tag or publication is authorized by this audit.

The audit began at local Glippy revision
`e06ffc160bc19fb432ef77bc9da523e7a99fb8e6`. At that point local `main` was 56
commits ahead of `origin/main`. Both public repositories,
`faustbrian/gox` and `faustbrian/glippy`, exposed `main` at
`7cfcaf4b06484c09ddbcdfd6e80284b52eed4d84`.

## Remaining Product Boundaries

The following are useful continuations but do not invalidate the implemented
v0.5 engineering contracts:

- cgo-generated inputs conservatively fall back to complete LSP package loads;
- runtime custom analyzers remain intentionally unsupported until a stable,
  statically compiled extension contract is justified;
- network and userspace filesystems and forced power-loss durability remain
  outside the supported atomic write/fix boundary; and
- newer interprocedural aliases, dynamic dispatch, recursion, and ambiguous
  package variants remain conservative rather than producing speculative
  correctness findings.

## Exit Requirements

The v0.5 milestone can close only after the corrected candidate passes safe
local verification, fresh independent source review, and exact-revision CI.
Aggregate process-tree RSS and runtime process-containment claims remain
outside the milestone until a non-disruptive evidence policy exists. The
maintainer does not own routine implementation or review. A tag or release is
still prohibited until the complete candidate has been verified and the
maintainer separately authorizes publication.
