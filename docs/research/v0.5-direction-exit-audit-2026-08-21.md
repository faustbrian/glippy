# v0.5 Direction Exit Audit, 2026-08-21

## Decision

The local implementation has completed the seven engineering stages in the
v0.5 Clippy/PHPStan direction: typed-memory reduction, incremental workspace
analysis, multi-target analysis, project semantic contracts, state-transition
correctness, curated strictness profiles, and deliberate opt-in catalog growth.

The earlier delivery evidence is retained below, but successive independent
reviews of the subsequent candidate found material gaps. Candidate `2571025`
corrected bounded LSP supersession and shutdown, external replacement
invalidation, conservative RWMutex and WaitGroup reasoning, target-matrix
suppression and partial-result accounting, baseline and changed-code policy on
incomplete output, catalog admission, and historical evidence attribution.
Candidate `a4de9b3` then aligned the CLI compatibility fixture with the
conservative WaitGroup proof boundary. Safe local verification, a final
source-only review with no findings, and exact-revision CI `32547111862` close
the bounded v0.5 engineering milestone. Runtime RSS, signal, interruption, and
descendant-termination probes are excluded because they are not safe to
execute on the development host; aggregate RSS and process-containment
behavior therefore remain unproven. No tag or publication is authorized by
this audit for v0.5.

The audit began at local Glippy revision
`e06ffc160bc19fb432ef77bc9da523e7a99fb8e6`. At that point local `main` was 56
commits ahead of `origin/main`. Both public repositories,
`faustbrian/gox` and `faustbrian/glippy`, exposed `main` at
`7cfcaf4b06484c09ddbcdfd6e80284b52eed4d84`.

## Remaining Product Boundaries

The following are useful continuations but do not invalidate the implemented
v0.5 engineering contracts:

- cgo-generated inputs conservatively fall back to complete LSP package loads;
- runtime custom analyzers remain intentionally unsupported until a stable,
  statically compiled extension contract is justified;
- network and userspace filesystems and forced power-loss durability remain
  outside the supported atomic write/fix boundary; and
- newer interprocedural aliases, dynamic dispatch, recursion, and ambiguous
  package variants remain conservative rather than producing speculative
  correctness findings.

## Exit Result

The bounded v0.5 engineering milestone is closed at corrected code candidate
`a4de9b3`: safe local verification passed, the final source-only review reported
no findings, and exact-revision CI passed. Aggregate process-tree RSS, signals,
interruption, descendant cleanup, and runtime process-containment claims remain
outside the milestone and unproven. The refreshed external complete-package
gate also remains outside current release evidence because no immutable result
artifact was retained. The maintainer does not own routine implementation or
review. No v0.5 tag or release has been created, and publication remains
prohibited until the complete product is genuinely ready and the maintainer
has reviewed it and explicitly authorizes publication.
