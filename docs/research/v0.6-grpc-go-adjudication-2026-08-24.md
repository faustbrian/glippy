## Findings

The actionable findings are:

- seven `nilness` diagnostics: one redundant non-nil guard, one repeated stale
  error check, and five stale error checks after `xdsclient.NewPool`;
- two `suspicious-range` diagnostics that clear interceptors only on copies of
  range values and leave the stored route values unchanged; and
- five discarded `bufio.Writer.Flush` errors in the end-to-end test harness.

The three `unreachable-code` findings are final `return` statements immediately
after `testing.T.Fatalf`. They are compatibility shims for the compiler's view
of testing termination, not defective unreachable work. The general testing
no-return precision correction in `972cfcf` removes them without suppressing
ordinary unreachable statements.

## Comparator Coverage

Go vet completed without findings. Staticcheck is dominated by API deprecation
and style-policy findings. Its three SA4006 findings are final `idInRPC++`
increments after the last use. They are harmless ineffective assignments and
do not establish a strong new default-rule candidate from this repository.

## Decision

Retain the existing nilness, range-copy mutation, and writer-finalization rules
at their current profiles. No gRPC-Go-specific exception or new default rule is
justified. The exact corpus must rerun at the latest Glippy revision before this
adjudication contributes to the v0.6 exit evidence.
