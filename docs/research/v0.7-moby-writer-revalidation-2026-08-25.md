# v0.7 Moby Writer Revalidation, 2026-08-25

## Decision

Reproduce the three historical Moby `unchecked-writer-error` precision
fingerprints against pinned source shapes instead of carrying their v0.6
classification forward unchanged.

## Evidence

Moby revision `b612274c5489b546ff8b4a4f93f25a0b8952713a` contains one redundant
deferred `archive/tar.Writer.Close` followed by an observed close, and two
equivalent directory-formatting helpers. Each helper writes file name, mode,
size, and modification time through a `bufio.Writer` and `tabwriter.Writer`
chain backed by `bytes.Buffer`.

The exact-shape characterization proves the current rule excludes the pinned
tar source shape. It initially reproduced both directory-helper shapes because
`io/fs.FileMode` has a standard-library `String` method and was conservatively
treated like an arbitrary formatting callback.

## Precision Contract

Exact `io/fs.FileMode` formatting is now an authoritative non-callback value,
alongside basic values and exact `time.Time`. Its scalar standard-library
formatting cannot capture or reinitialize the destination writer. User-defined
formatting or error method sets, interfaces, and other dynamic values remain
conservative because their methods may mutate or expose the writer.

The focused characterization covers the nested bufio and tabwriter chain and
both `Flush` calls. The complete pinned Moby corpus remains a v0.8 exact-rerun
gate before the historical fingerprints are removed from canonical corpus
evidence.
