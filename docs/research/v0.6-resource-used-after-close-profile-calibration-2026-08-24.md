# v0.6 `resource-used-after-close` Profile Calibration, 2026-08-24

## Decision

Move `resource-used-after-close` from `suspicious` to `nursery` and remove it
from the curated `recommended` profile. The rule remains available through the
explicit nursery preset or an exact rule override.

## Evidence

The adjudicated v0.6 corpus produced five recommended findings for this rule:
one in containerd and four in gRPC-Go. All five are intentional closed-state
contract tests, and none is a true positive. The containerd case reads a custom
`io.ReadCloser` after `Close` to verify that its implementation translates a
closed pipe into `io.EOF`. The gRPC-Go cases read or write closed pipes and
connections to verify their documented terminal behavior.

The control-flow model correctly proves that each operation follows `Close`.
The unsupported inference is that every such operation is defective. Go's
`Close` convention does not standardize post-close behavior, and dynamic
interface dispatch can hide a concrete implementation that deliberately
defines it. Test-file exclusions or assertion-library matching would suppress
unrelated defects without proving intent.

## Observable Contract

The built-in `default`, `recommended`, `strict`, and `pedantic` profiles do not
select `resource-used-after-close`. `presets = ["nursery"]` and an explicit
`resource-used-after-close = "warn"` or `"error"` override continue to run the
existing analyzer without changing its diagnostic or suppression contract.

## Revisit Trigger

Promote the rule only after corpus evidence demonstrates useful precision and
the implementation can distinguish defective operations from intentional
post-close contract validation through general semantic evidence. Do not
promote it through test-path, function-name, or assertion-library heuristics.
